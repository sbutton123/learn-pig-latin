How Do You Say Happy Birthday in Pig Latin?

Happy Birthday↓Appy-hay Irthday-bay!

Happy Birthday to you!↓Appy-hay Irthday-bay oo-tay oo-yay!

Move the starting consonant sound to the end and add “ay”: happy becomes appyhay, and birthday becomes irthdaybay. The hyphens here help you say it aloud. For words starting with a vowel, our translator adds “yay.”
