Why two versions?

Pig Latin has different variations. Some speakers add YAY to words beginning with a vowel, while others add WAY. Both are used. In Piglatinia, we call them the Northern and Southern dialects.

— sent from LearnPigLatin.com

How the translator works

  • English to Pig Latin: words that start with a vowel get YAY in the Northern dialect or WAY in the Southern dialect (apple → appleyay or appleway). Words that start with consonants move them to the end and add "ay" (hello → ellohay, string → ingstray).
  • Pig Latin to English: the translator un-flips your words and makes its best guess. Pig Latin can be sneaky, so a few words have twins. "Owhay" could be how or who!
  • Numbers, punctuation, and web addresses stay just the way you typed them.
